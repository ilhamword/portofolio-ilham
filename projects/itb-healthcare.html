Challenge & Context

The Problem

UPT Layanan Kesehatan ITB (Bumi Medika Ganesha / BMG) serves tens of thousands of active students, lecturers, academic staff, and their families across Ganesha and Jatinangor campuses. During peak academic medical check-up (MCU) cycles and routine consultations, the legacy system experienced severe booking concurrency bottlenecks, slot overbooking, and synchronization delays with national health insurance services.

The operational challenge required modernizing the reservation pipeline with strict transactional concurrency to prevent race conditions during slot bookings, automating real-time participant eligibility checks via the BPJS PCare API, and establishing enterprise single sign-on (SSO) with Microsoft Azure AD while maintaining complete audit trails across 73,000+ patient records.

Institutional Bottlenecks
High-concurrency race conditions leading to quota overbooking during peak morning hours
Manual patient BPJS verification causing long queues at on-site clinic reception
Disjointed multi-profile dependencies for academic staff families and dependents

Concurrency & Atomic Quota

To prevent overbooking during high-demand doctor shifts, slot allocations are wrapped in transactional atomic queries with pessimistic row locking. If multiple users attempt to reserve the final remaining quota simultaneously, requests are serialized safely without data corruption.

BPJS Signature & API Fallback

Bridging communications with BPJS PCare use cryptographic HMAC authorization headers generated per-request with UTC timestamp validation. Network timeouts or service downtimes trigger automated retry queues and graceful offline logging to prevent user booking blockage.

Normalized 47-Entity Schema

Structured 47 relational tables separating identity (`pengguna`), patient medical masters (`pasien`), family hierarchies (`keluarga_pasien`), doctor shifts (`shift`, `jadwal_layanan`), and transactional event logs (`log_reservasi`, `log_reservasi_mcu`, `log_srq20`) to maintain 3NF normalization.
