Fintech Security Context

The High Stakes of Digital Identity Fraud

Financial institutions and fintech startups face severe compliance penalties and multi-billion rupiah credit default risks when fraudsters submit fabricated KTP images, stolen national identities, or replayed video selfies.

Legacy OCR tools frequently hallucinate indistinct digits on weathered Indonesian national ID cards, causing legitimate borrowers to be rejected while sophisticated Photoshop edits slip through without forensic examination.

Critical Banking Vulnerabilities
Replay attacks using printed photos or smartphone screens during video selfies
Stolen identity numbers (NIK) with altered physical birthdates and altered names
Digital manipulation and splice tampering of identity card photos
Manual reviewer fatigue resulting in high turnaround time (>24 hours)

Digits 1–6: Administrative Geography

Encodes Province (1-2), Regency/City (3-4), and District (5-6). Cross-referenced against the official Kemendagri spatial gazetteer to prevent fictional address codes.

Digits 7–8: Birth Date & Gender Offset

Male dates range from 01–31. Female dates receive a statutory mathematical offset of +40 (41–71). The system decrypts the offset to infer gender and verify exact day of birth.

Digits 9–10: Month of Birth

Validates statutory Gregorian calendar range (01–12). Any value outside this range triggers an immediate automated fraud alert.

Digits 11–12: Year of Birth (YY)

Extracts two-digit birth year, cross-compared with extracted OCR document text to detect altered age or falsified physical documents.

Digits 13–16: Computerized Registration

System serial number generated chronologically by Dukcapil, ensuring unique population identification.
